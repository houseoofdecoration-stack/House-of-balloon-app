Privacy Policy – House of Balloons

House of Balloons provides balloon decoration services (birthday, anniversary, baby shower, welcome baby and other events) in Delhi NCR.

What we collect

When you fill our Facebook/Instagram lead form or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you share: name, phone number, email, city, event type, event date and your messages.

How we use it

Only to reply to your inquiry, share quotes and decoration options, and manage your booking. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Storage

Your details are stored securely in our internal customer management system (Google Firebase) and are accessible only to the House of Balloons team.

Delete your data

To have your data deleted, email houseoofdecoration@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp with "Delete my data". We will delete it within 7 days.
